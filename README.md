Palmera Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game: explore a 2,600×2,600 city on foot or by car, steal cars, outrun the PBPD police as your wanted level rises, and complete 4 story missions — playable offline in your browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
